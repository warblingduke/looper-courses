# Course names inside multi-course facilities (2026-10-04)

484 courses renamed across 258 facility packs. Course ids, holes and geometry are
unchanged; only the `name` field and the matching manifest hashes changed.

- 472 courses that carried a plain descriptor ("Bethpage State Park Golf Courses ·
  18-hole course") now carry their real name ("Bethpage State Park · Green Course").
- 12 names had a broken facility prefix ("Brunswick Plantation & · Magnolia Course"
  → "Brunswick Plantation · Magnolia Course").

A name was assigned only on decisive evidence tied to that course's own holes:
OpenStreetMap course tags on the hole ways (331), a named OpenStreetMap course
outline holding the course's greens (31), or an OpenGolfAPI scorecard (ODbL) whose
hole-by-hole pars match the course and no sibling course (110). Courses without
such evidence keep their honest descriptor.

Generated by `tools/foundry/walk_names.py` in the app repo; per-course provenance in
`docs/walk-names-2026-10-04/renamed.csv`.
