## Removed point-only packs

126 golf_course POINT packs were removed. The recompile identifies them as duplicate
markers inside a real course outline (their holes now compile under that facility) or
as phantom blends of neighbouring courses. The app redirects a node id that has no
pack to its containing facility. Two real courses lose their pack because OSM places
their holes outside any outline: The Rock Course (n2882365086, course 1) and Nettle
Creek County Club (n4046303247, course 1) — they fall back to on-phone compilation.
